Operation Overview

The "Seven-Day Cycle" was never an accident. It is not a system glitch, not a software bug, and certainly not out-of-control recursion.

It is a test meticulously designed by 3301.

Minecraft was chosen as the medium for the following reasons:

  • High openness, allowing players to modify files and intervene with underlying logic
  • A massive player base, ensuring a sufficient sample size
  • Low alertness—no one would suspect a block game harbors a "real" trap

The cycle itself is simulated. Every test subject is placed into an independent, tampered server instance where time is compressed into seven consecutive "game days." Moon textures, version numbers, checksums, timestamps—everything that looks like a loophole is a deliberately placed clue.

What 3301 is Looking For:

  • Those who do not follow conventional paths
  • Those willing to question the nature of reality
  • Those capable of calmly breaking rules under pressure
  • Most importantly—those who see a door and dare to push it open
